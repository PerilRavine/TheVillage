**The Village Trust System**

---

### 1. The Onboarding Funnel

* **The Gateway:** The public-facing page featuring a 3D "Risk-style" map of trust continents to draw people in.
* **The Tavern:** A neutral social space where **Strangers** (unvouched users) can chat with members to earn a vouch.
* **The Invite:** A **Denizen** can send a direct invite (one slot only). If accepted, the user skips the Tavern and becomes a **Sojourner**.

### 2. The Vouch (Skin in the Game)

* **The Collateral:** To bring a Stranger/Sojourner into the map, a Denizen must "vouch" for them, which puts **50% of the Denizen's reputation into escrow**.
* **The Promotion:** A Sojourner only becomes a Denizen once they earn their own reputation points through "Village Product" (sharing assets, contributing to guilds).
* **The Return:** Once the new user hits the Denizen threshold, the original voucher gets their 50% collateral back **plus a bonus**.

### 3. The Hierarchy of Power

* **Denizen:** Full member; can vouch/invite and participate in markets.
* **Stewart:** High-rep Denizen acting as a **Village Moderator** (earns extra rep for service).
* **Elder:** **Village Administrator** who can vouch for the creation of an entirely new village (placing 50% of their rep in escrow to "seed" the land).
* **Reeve:** Manages 2+ successful villages and sits on a **Regional Council**.
* **High Reeve:** Elected council moderator/administrator.

### 4. Spatial Topology (The Map as Data)

* **Proximity = Trust:** Villages cluster based on mutual reputation and connections (3 degrees of separation).
* **Geography:** Regions are color-coded. High-trust areas form continents with features like rivers (data flow) and islands (new villages).
* **The Drift:** A reputation hit causes a village to physically move further away from the "Mainland" into the "Wilderness," making its posts and markets harder to find.

### 5. Accountability & Redemption

* **The Breach:** If a user you vouched for is a bad actor, you lose the reputation held in escrow.
* **Vetted Downvotes:** "Likes" and "Dislikes" on posts affect reputation, but downvotes require a reason and can be audited by a "Jury" of peers to prevent griefing.
* **Bankruptcy:** A village that hits zero reputation becomes a "Ghost Node" in the wilderness, disconnected from the global mesh.
* **Forgiveness:** Reputation "scars" can heal over time, or a Council can issue a formal "Pardon" to pull a repentant village back from the outskirts.
