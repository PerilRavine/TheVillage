That makes total sense. Keeping the **Village** as a pure social architecture ensures that the trust mechanics and the "Risk" map can stand on their own without the complexity of the underlying server engine.

Here is the refined, "clean room" summary of the **Trust System** and the **UI**, stripped of everything but the core Village experience.

---

### **The Trust System (The Social Engine)**

* **Vouching & Collateral:** To enter the map, a **Stranger** must be vouched for by a **Denizen**. This puts **50% of the Denizen's reputation** into an escrow "bond."
* **Promotion & Return:** Once the newcomer (Sojourner) earns enough reputation through community contribution, they are promoted to Denizen. At that moment, the original mentor gets their 50% back **plus a bonus**.
* **One-Slot Invites:** A Denizen has exactly **one** invite slot for external friends. The reputation lock only triggers if the invite is accepted.
* **The Hierarchy:**
* **Stewarts:** Moderators who earn extra rep for keeping the peace.
* **Elders:** Administrators who can "seed" entirely new villages by staking their reputation.
* **Reeves:** Leaders who manage multiple successful villages and form **Regional Councils**.


* **Spatial Bankruptcy:** If a village's reputation hits zero, it drifts into the "Wilderness" and becomes a **Ghost Node**, physically disconnected from the mainland.

---

### **The UI (The Spatial Narrative)**

* **The Gateway & Tavern:** The public entry point. The Gateway shows the global map to draw people in; the Tavern is the social lobby where Strangers and Denizens meet for handshakes.
* **The "Risk" Map:** A 3D, procedural world where **Distance = Trust**.
* **Continents:** High-trust clusters named by their presiding Councils.
* **Features:** Rivers (high-activity pathways), Islands (new colonies), and desaturated "Fog" for the wilderness.
* **Color-Coding:** Distinct colors for different Council jurisdictions.


* **The Domicile (Your Home Base):**
* **Personal Space:** A private 3D environment that acts as your workspace.
* **Control Center:** This is where you access your toolsets, manage your "Invite" slot, and monitor your personal reputation ledger.
* **Windows to the World:** Feeds and messages from the villages you belong to are aggregated here. Looking "out" of your Domicile shows you the actual state of the village you are currently living in.


* **The Feed:** A proximity-based aggregator. It only shows you what is relevant within **3 degrees of separation**, keeping the noise low and the trust high.

---

### **The Integrity Loop**

The beauty of this is its simplicity: **Social value creates physical land.**

If you treat people well, your continent grows, your rivers flow, and you move toward the center of the world. If trust is breached, your land literally breaks away and drifts into the dark. It’s a self-correcting, visual representation of human integrity.
