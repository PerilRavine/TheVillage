Business Concept: The Village (P2P Trust Network)
Lead Architect: Bruce Flett

1. Executive Summary
The Village is a decentralized resource-sharing platform built for the geographical and social landscape of Northern BC. It allows neighbors to exchange high-value resources and skills through a verified trust protocol, prioritizing data ethics and community resilience over corporate advertising.

2. Technical Capability (The "Proven Record")
The project is led by Bruce Flett, a veteran Software Architect with a documented history of building scalable, international-grade platforms, most notably:

Lead Developer at Codesyne (2013-2014): Architected modular systems for commercial markets.

Architect of Moduleblocks: Designed the core structural logic for a platform that secured federal innovation support.

Full-Stack Expertise: Expert in Django, Laravel, and modern frontend frameworks, currently utilizing agentic AI (Cline/Gemini) to minimize development overhead.

3. The Pilot Goal
Infrastructure: 3-server distributed environment (currently being scaffolded in Docker).

User Base: A pilot cell of 5 high-trust users in the Northern BC region to validate the "Trust Handshake" logic.
